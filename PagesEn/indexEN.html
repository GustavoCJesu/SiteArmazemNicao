A little about us

Armazéns Gerais Nicão is a reference in storage and logistics services in the Andradas region (MG). Founded in January 2019, the company specializes in offering complete storage solutions in logistics warehouses, with a focus on efficiency, safety and personalized service for each client.



Strategically located on the Andradas / Espírito Santo do Pinhal Highway, in the Contendas neighborhood, Nicão acts as an important partner for producers, distributors and companies looking for adequate infrastructure to store goods in an organized and safe way.
